# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Technical readers (engineers, DevOps people, developers) who find the site through search, Hacker News, or LinkedIn, and people who decide whether to hire or trust Rutger de Knijf. They scan on a laptop or a phone, often during work hours, and they decide fast whether he knows what he is talking about.

## Product Purpose

deknijf.com is the blog and professional home of Rutger de Knijf, an independent consultant (infrastructure, DevOps, Python, AI agents) who works through De Knijf ICT B.V. It exists to turn deep technical experience into visible authority and, in the end, consulting work. Success means that a reader recognizes real experience, reads on, subscribes, or gets in touch through the Work with me page.

## Positioning

Everything comes from first-hand work: systems Rutger built, runs, and writes about in his own voice. The free APIs on the Projects page are live, public, and open source, so a reader can check the claim directly.

## Operating Context

Readers arrive on a single post or page from an external link, and often leave after one screen. Posts get distributed to LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Dev.to, Hacker News, and a Buttondown newsletter.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hugo static site on PaperMod with custom layouts and CSS in `assets/css/extended/`. No npm and no JavaScript framework.
- Light and dark mode both ship; dark mode is the `body.dark` class.
- Copy must stay in Rutger's own words. Prose for publication is written by Claude Fable and passes `/humanizer`.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere in visible text.
- Unaffiliated services must keep their disclaimers: AD Feed Europe (EASA) and Notified Body Check (European Commission).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rutger de Knijf. Company: De Knijf ICT.
- Voice: direct, technical, dry humor, opinionated, no hedging. The reader is trusted, not lectured.
- Cover images are real museum photographs of sculpture.
- Anti-references: corporate tech blogs (stock photos, gradient CTAs, newsletter popups) and designer portfolios (flashy animation, typography experiments).

## Evidence on Hand

- Six live free APIs with public source: lateststable.org, Grid emissions, ISDE, zonecheck, AD Feed Europe, Notified Body Check (`content/projects.md`).
- Named client testimonials from Energyworx (`content/work-with-me.md`).
- Published posts in `content/posts/`.
- No customer logos, usage numbers, or benchmarks exist for the APIs. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real thing before any claim about it.
2. Content leads; design serves what is being read.
3. Honest over polished: no decoration without a job.
4. Publishing stays low-friction: new content must not need new design work.
